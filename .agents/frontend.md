# Lumen Frontend Instructions

## Design Direction

Lumen should feel like a calm operational dashboard:

- map-first where spatial context matters
- dense but readable
- serious, restrained, and trustworthy
- simple enough for non-technical municipal users
- closer to Uber's simplicity than heavy enterprise software
- situational-awareness patterns may be used, but avoid intimidating or surveillance-like UX

## Layout Rules

- Prefer one clear app shell with top navigation, municipality selection, module tabs, map/workspace area, detail panels, and summary metrics.
- Avoid marketing pages for core product surfaces.
- Avoid nested cards and decorative page sections.
- Use cards only for repeated items, contained metrics, module summaries, and focused panels.
- Stable dimensions matter for maps, toolbars, stat bars, tabs, and module cards.
- Mobile must not horizontally overflow.

## UI Rules

- Every visible score needs source, confidence, and limitation text nearby or one click away.
- Controls should be plain and predictable: tabs, segmented controls, switches, sliders, tables, and clear buttons.
- Do not add login, payments, marketplace flows, buyer-facing acquisition workflows, or lead-gen surfaces unless the product policy changes explicitly.
- Avoid decorative gradients, orbs, oversized hero sections, and generic SaaS marketing copy.

## Accessibility And Responsiveness

- Use semantic headings and labels.
- Keep keyboard focus states intact.
- Buttons need clear text or accessible labels.
- Text must wrap cleanly in Dutch and English.
- Verify at desktop and mobile widths after UI changes.
