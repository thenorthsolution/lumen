# Lumen Product Principles

## Mission

Lumen exists to make hidden housing capacity and delivery blockers visible in the Dutch housing market.

The first serious audience is municipal government and semi-public housing actors. The public layer should remain useful without login or payment.

## Hard Line

If information, data, or a feature helps understand the housing shortage and can be published safely, it should be free in a non-exploitable form.

Lumen must not become tooling for:

- buyer targeting
- resident or tenant pressure
- vulnerable-group segmentation
- speculative acquisition lists
- lead generation from public-interest signals
- exclusive hoarding of civic housing intelligence

## Paid Work Boundary

Future paid work may include:

- custom reports
- private deployments
- internal workflow tooling
- data cleaning and integration
- compliance-grade exports
- implementation support
- collaboration features for approved public-interest actors

Paid work must not restrict the public core or enable exploitative market asymmetry.

## Copy And Claims

- Use sober language.
- Prefer "signal", "proxy", "candidate", "shortlist", and "requires local verification".
- Avoid "available", "vacant", "buildable", "profitable", or "actionable" unless the data truly proves it.
- Legal and governance documents are drafts until reviewed by qualified Dutch/EU counsel.
