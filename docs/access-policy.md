# Lumen Access Policy

## Default Access

The public-interest layer should remain available without login or payment when publication is safe.

## Professional Access

Professional features may be offered for approved public-interest work, including:

- custom municipal reports
- private deployments
- collaboration workflows
- data cleaning and integration
- compliance-grade exports
- implementation support

## Review Rights

Lumen should reserve the right to review, restrict, suspend, or terminate access when use conflicts with the Public Interest Charter or Data Use Policy.

## No Extractive Access Tier

No access tier should enable buyer targeting, resident targeting, pressure campaigns, speculative acquisition lists, or exclusive control over public-interest housing signals.

## Legal Status

This policy is a product governance draft and should be converted into enforceable terms by qualified Dutch/EU legal counsel.
