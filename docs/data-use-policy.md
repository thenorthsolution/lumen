# Lumen Data Use Policy

## Allowed Uses

Lumen data and features may be used to:

- understand vacancy, underuse, land opportunity, and delivery bottlenecks
- support municipal planning, policy analysis, and public accountability
- prepare public-interest conversations between governments, housing corporations, researchers, journalists, and civic organizations
- validate or challenge Lumen's methodology and source assumptions
- identify records requiring local verification or correction

## Prohibited Uses

Lumen data and features may not be used to:

- target buyers, residents, tenants, or vulnerable groups
- pressure owners or residents based on unverified proxy signals
- build lead-generation, acquisition, or speculative targeting lists
- represent Lumen scores as confirmed vacancy, legal buildability, or investment advice
- resell public-interest insight in a way that reduces public visibility
- combine Lumen data with private datasets to create exploitative market advantage

## Publication Standard

Published Lumen outputs should include source, confidence, methodology, and limitations. Proxy signals must remain clearly labeled as proxy signals.

## Legal Status

This policy is a product governance draft and should be reviewed by qualified Dutch/EU legal counsel.
