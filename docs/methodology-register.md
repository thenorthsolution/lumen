# Lumen Methodology Register

The methodology register tracks every visible score, signal, confidence level, source, and limitation used by Lumen.

## Vacancy Signal

- Module: Lumen Vacancy
- Sources: BAG verblijfsobjecten and panden via PDOK; official permit-publication context where available
- Purpose: identify possible vacant or underused buildings suitable for follow-up research
- Confidence: medium for shortlist relevance; low for confirmed vacancy
- Limitation: BAG status is administrative and does not confirm real-world vacancy

## Space Signal

- Module: Lumen Space
- Sources: BAG usage attributes, Ruimtelijkeplannen.nl, municipality boundaries through PDOK
- Purpose: identify underused land, infill opportunities, and transformation sites
- Confidence: medium for first screening; low for legal buildability
- Limitation: zoning, ownership, environmental constraints, and design feasibility require local verification

## Pipeline Signal

- Module: Lumen Pipeline
- Sources: TenderNed, official publications, regional heuristics
- Purpose: show public construction activity, permit/tender pressure, and possible delivery bottlenecks
- Confidence: low to medium
- Limitation: TenderNed does not cover the full private housing market and may lag real delivery activity

## Required Display Standard

Every surfaced opportunity should show:

- source
- confidence
- reason for inclusion
- known limitations
- local verification requirement

## Legal Status

This register is an operational transparency document, not a legal or investment opinion.
