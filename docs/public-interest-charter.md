# Lumen Public Interest Charter

Lumen exists to improve visibility, coordination, and accountability in the Dutch housing market.

## Public Core

Information that materially helps the public understand housing capacity, vacancy, land opportunity, delivery constraints, methodology, and data limitations should remain free when it can be published safely.

Lumen should not hide civic housing intelligence behind a paywall when that information contributes to public understanding of the housing shortage.

## Non-Exploitation Commitment

Lumen should not be used to:

- pressure residents, tenants, buyers, or vulnerable groups
- target private individuals for speculative acquisition or lead generation
- create private asymmetry from public-interest housing signals
- hoard opportunity data that should be visible to public actors
- present proxy signals as verified vacancy or legal development rights

## Professional Services Boundary

Revenue, if needed, should come from non-extractive work such as custom reports, private deployments, data cleaning, compliance exports, workflow support, and implementation guidance for approved public-interest actors.

Professional access must not restrict the public core or convert Lumen into a buyer-targeting, resident-targeting, or speculative acquisition platform.

## Legal Status

This charter is a product and governance draft. Qualified Dutch/EU legal counsel should convert it into enforceable terms before institutional deployment.
