# Lumen Responsible Disclosure And Data Correction Policy

Lumen uses public and administrative datasets. Those datasets can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong. Corrections are part of the product, not an exception.

## Who Can Report

Reports may come from municipalities, housing corporations, owners, residents, buyers, journalists, researchers, or civic organizations.

## What Can Be Reported

- incorrect object classification
- outdated status
- misleading score or explanation
- missing source limitation
- privacy, safety, or misuse concern
- suspected prohibited use of Lumen outputs

## Expected Handling

Lumen should:

- acknowledge serious reports promptly
- review source evidence and local context
- correct or annotate records where appropriate
- preserve methodology transparency
- avoid exposing reporters to retaliation or pressure

## Limits

Lumen cannot independently verify every real-world property condition. When uncertainty remains, records should be marked with a limitation instead of being presented as fact.

## Legal Status

This policy is a governance draft and should be reviewed by qualified Dutch/EU legal counsel before institutional deployment.
